# Why Hammerspoon? A Guide for Non-Technical Team Members

## The Problem We're Solving

Every day, our team spends valuable time on repetitive tasks that slow us down:

- **Window Management Chaos**: Manually dragging windows to the right positions, switching between multiple monitors, and organizing our workspace takes minutes every day
- **Application Switching Overhead**: Finding and switching between applications, projects, and windows interrupts our flow
- **Context Switching Costs**: Losing track of which project we're working on, where files are located, and what we were doing
- **Inconsistent Workflows**: Each team member has different ways of organizing their workspace, making collaboration harder

These small interruptions add up. Research shows that it takes an average of **23 minutes** to fully refocus after an interruption. When you multiply that by dozens of interruptions per day, we're losing hours of productive work time.

## What Hammerspoon Does

Hammerspoon is a powerful automation tool for macOS that acts like a **smart assistant for your computer**. Think of it as having a personal productivity system that:

- **Remembers your workspace layouts** and restores them instantly
- **Launches applications and projects** with a single keystroke
- **Manages windows intelligently** across multiple monitors
- **Tracks your active projects** so you always know what you're working on
- **Automates repetitive tasks** that you do dozens of times per day

Instead of clicking through menus, dragging windows, and searching for files, you press a few keys and everything happens instantly.
